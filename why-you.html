✦ 04   The Honest Truth

Because I Choose You

I'm not saying this because it's easy to say. I'm saying this because I mean it, and because you deserve to hear it — clearly, without confusion. I choose you. Not as a last option, not by accident. I choose you deliberately. I choose to show up, to notice, to care, to try. And I will keep choosing you, as many times as I need to.

— Ayush
