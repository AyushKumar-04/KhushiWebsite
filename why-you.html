✦   From Ayush, to Khushi   ✦

Why You

I have tried to find one perfect reason for why you became so important to me. I couldn't. Because it wasn't one thing.

Read on

✦ The Tiny Things

It was a hundred tiny things that happened without either of us announcing that something was changing.

It was meeting you.
It was talking to you for hours.
It was learning the little things you do without realizing you do them.

It was the way you laugh.
The way you look at things.
The way you say “ayu.”
The way you can be completely stubborn about something and then, five minutes later, make me smile without even trying.

It was your glasses.
Your smile.
Your weird little habits.
The things you love.
The things you don't like.
The way you get irritated.
The way you suddenly become soft.
The way you can say “I love you” in the middle of a completely normal conversation and somehow make my entire mood change.

I didn't fall for one version of you.
I fell for all of it.

✦ 01   The Beginning

Because You Happened

I didn't plan to fall for you.

There wasn't some moment where I sat down and decided, “This is the person I want.”

It just happened.

Somewhere between the conversations, the jokes, the late-night thoughts, the little things you told me, and the moments we started sharing, you stopped being just another person in my life.

You became someone I started looking for.
Someone whose name could change my entire mood.
Someone I wanted to tell things to.
Someone whose happiness started mattering to me in a way I couldn't explain.

And that's when I realized something had changed. You had become home in a very strange way.

Not because everything with you was always easy. It wasn't. But because even through all the confusion, there was something about you that kept pulling my heart back.

— Ayush

✦ 02   Effortless

Because You Don't Try

You don't have to try to be beautiful. You just are.
You don't have to try to be interesting. You just are.

And the funny thing is, I don't think you even realize how many little things about you stay in my head.

Sometimes I remember something you said hours after we talked.
Sometimes I'll see something completely unrelated and immediately think, “She would like this.”
Sometimes I'll look at one of our photos and smile for no reason.

That's what you did to me. You became part of the way I see ordinary things.

— Ayush

✦ 03   The Details

Because of Every Little Thing

I don't think people understand how much the little things can mean.

The way you adjust your glasses.
The way you laugh.
The way you look sideways when you're thinking.
The way you get excited about the things you love.
Your favourite food. Your bike rides. Your obsession with cute things. Flowers.

The little expressions on your face that probably mean nothing to you but somehow mean everything to me.

Even the things that annoy me sometimes became things I learned to recognize as simply… you.

And I don't want to love some imaginary perfect version of you. I want to know the real you.

The happy you. The irritated you. The confused you. The stubborn you. The quiet you.
The version of you that doesn't know what she wants.
The version that needs space.
The version that wants to be held.

All of you.
— Ayush

“You were surprised. You hugged me. You asked me why.”

✦ 04   The Flowers & The Surprise

Because of That Day

There is one memory I don't think I'll ever forget.

The day I came with the flowers.

I didn't come because I wanted to create some perfect movie scene. I came because I wanted to do something that said what I couldn't properly say with words.

I wanted you to know:
“I thought about you.”
“I wanted to see you smile.”
“I wanted to make one ordinary day feel a little different.”

And when I finally saw you, holding that bouquet and seeing your reaction, something inside me became very quiet.

You were surprised.
You hugged me.
You asked me why.

And somewhere in that moment, I realized that I didn't actually need a complicated answer.

I came because it was you.
Because when someone becomes important to you, sometimes you just want to show up.
— Ayush

“No analysis. No fear. No questions. Just us.”

✦ 05   The Memory

Because of the Temple, the Rain & Us

I think about that day more than you probably know.

The temple.
The rain.
Walking together.
Holding your hand.
Those little moments where everything around us seemed to disappear for a while.

And our first photos together.

Maybe they're just photographs to somebody else. To me, they're proof that for a little while, two people who had been through so much confusion were simply happy to be together.

No analysis.
No fear.
No questions.
Just us.

And I will always be grateful that I got to experience that with you.

— Ayush

“I want him too.” — That's the kind of choice I want from you.

✦ 06   The Honest Choice

Because I Choose You

I'm not writing this to convince you.

I'm not writing this so you feel guilty.

I'm not writing this because I expect anything from you.

I'm writing it because you deserve to know what you became to me.

I chose you.
Not because there weren't other people in the world.
Not because I was afraid I'd never find anyone else.
Not because you were convenient.

I chose you because something about you reached a part of me that I didn't know was waiting to be reached.

And even when things became complicated, that feeling didn't simply disappear.

But I've also learned something important. Loving someone doesn't mean holding them tightly enough that they can't leave. Sometimes loving someone means giving them enough space to choose you freely.

And if you ever choose me, I want it to be because you genuinely want to. Not because I convinced you. Not because you felt guilty. Not because you were scared of hurting me.

Just because, after everything, your heart genuinely says:
“I want him too.”

That's the kind of choice I want from you.
And that's the kind of love I want to give you.

— Ayush
