✦ Confession 01

I think about you more than I say

There are moments in the middle of the day — completely random, completely ordinary — where you just appear in my mind. A photo, something you said, the way you laughed. And I stop for a second. Just a second. And then I keep going. But I thought of you. I think of you a lot.

— Ayush

✦ Confession 02

That photo of us — the desk one

That photo of our hands under the desk. I've looked at it more times than I should admit. There's something about it — the way it's hidden, the way it's real, the way it's ours — that hits me differently every time. I don't know. It just does something to me.

— Ayush

✦ Confession 03

The card you made — "I love you, Bayu"

The card with the lipstick marks and the "I love you" written everywhere — with "Bayu" at the bottom. I look at that and I feel something that's very hard to put into words. It's warmth. It's weight. It's this feeling that something real is happening here, and I want to protect it completely.

— Ayush

✦ Confession 04

I'm not letting go

I want to be very clear about this one. I'm not confused. I'm not uncertain. I know what I want. I want mornings where I get to say good morning to you. I want to know what your favourite version of yourself looks like. I want to show up when things are hard. I'm not letting go. Not unless you ask me to. And maybe not even then.

— Ayush

She made this. I keep it safe.
