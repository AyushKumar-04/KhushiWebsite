✦   Things I Needed To Say   ✦

Con fession

There are some things I don't know how to say when I'm talking to you.
Because when I'm actually with you, I usually end up saying something stupid, making a joke, or just looking at you and forgetting what I was going to say.

So I'm writing them here.

Read on
“I don't want my love to become a cage for you...
I want a version where we both choose each other. Every day. Freely.”
— Ayush

✦ Confession 01

I Think About You More Than I Say

You appear in my head at the most random times.

I'll be doing something completely normal and suddenly remember your face.

Something you said.
A photo.
A joke.
The way you laughed.
A moment that probably lasted five seconds but somehow stayed with me for days.

And sometimes I don't even want to admit how often it happens.

But it does.
I think about you. A lot.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 02

The Bouquet Wasn't Just a Bouquet

I want you to understand this one.

When I came with those flowers, I wasn't trying to impress you.
I wasn't trying to prove that I'm a better boyfriend.
I wasn't trying to create some grand gesture so you would owe me something.

I just wanted to give you something beautiful because, in my head, you deserved a beautiful moment.

And when I saw your reaction, I remember thinking:

“This is exactly why I came.”

Your smile made everything worth it.
The whole journey. The planning. The nervousness. Everything.

Because for those few hours, I wasn't thinking about the future. I wasn't thinking about what might happen. I was just happy that I was there with you.

— Ayush

“Your smile made everything worth it.”

✦ Confession 03

That Day Is One of My Favourite Memories

The temple.
The rain.
Your hand in mine.
The little moments between us.
The photos.
The feeling of walking beside you.
The way everything felt simple for a while.

I wish I could explain what that felt like without making it sound dramatic.

It wasn't some huge cinematic moment. It was actually the opposite.

It was quiet. Simple. Real.
And that's why I remember it.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 04

Your “I Love You, Ayu”

You once made me that card.

“I love you, Ayu.”
With the lipstick marks. With all those little details.

I don't think you understand what that meant to me.

I kept it because it wasn't just paper. It was a moment where you gave me something that came from you.

And every time I see it, I remember that there was a time when we weren't trying to figure everything out. We were just two people who loved each other.

And I don't want to erase that memory just because things later became complicated.

— Ayush

“It wasn't just paper. It came from you.”

✦ Confession 05

I Need to Confess Something Else

I know I've sometimes loved you from a place of fear.

Fear that I'll lose you.
Fear that someone else will become more important to you.
Fear that you'll wake up one day and realize you don't want me anymore.

And sometimes that fear made me behave in ways I don't like.

I asked too many questions.
I wanted too much reassurance.
I called too much.
I wanted to know what was happening because not knowing scared me.

I sometimes confused loving you with needing to know everything about you.

And I'm learning the difference.

I don't want my love to become a cage for you.
I don't want you to feel like you have to answer every call because you're afraid I'll fall apart.
I don't want you to feel responsible for keeping me calm.
That's not the kind of love I want to give you.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 06

I Don't Want to Fix You

I used to think that if you were hurting, I had to fix it.
If you were scared, I had to make the fear disappear.
If you were confused, I had to give you the answer.
If your past hurt you, I wanted to somehow make your past stop hurting.

But I finally understand something.

You're not a problem I need to solve.
You're a person I love.

And sometimes the best thing I can do is simply listen.

Sometimes I need to give you space.
Sometimes I need to let you figure things out without trying to pull the answer out of you.

— Ayush

✦ Confession 07

I Know You're Confused

And I don't want to pretend I don't know that.

I know there are things inside you that you're still trying to understand.
I know your past still affects you.
I know sometimes you can be close to me and suddenly feel overwhelmed.
I know you can tell me you love me and then feel like you need distance.

I've seen that contradiction.

And honestly? It has confused me too.

But I don't want to turn your confusion into something I use against you.

I just want honesty. That's all.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 08

If You Choose Me

I don't need some perfect relationship.
I don't need you to call me every hour.
I don't need you to never get irritated.
I don't need you to become someone you're not.

I just need honesty.

If you're happy, tell me.
If you're hurt, tell me.
If you need space, tell me.
If something is bothering you, tell me.
If you don't want this anymore, tell me.

And if you choose me, choose me because you actually want to build something with me.
Not because I waited long enough.
Not because I convinced you.
Not because you feel sorry for me.
Because you want me.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 09

And If You Don't Choose Me

This is probably the hardest thing for me to write.

But I need you to know that I understand.

I will never want your love because you were forced into it. I would rather have your honest “no” than a “yes” that your heart doesn't mean.

Of course it would hurt.
I'd probably think about you for a long time.
I'd miss you.
I'd remember everything.

But I would rather remember something real than hold onto something that you no longer want.
— Ayush

✦ Confession 10

My Real Confession

I don't know exactly what our future looks like.
I don't know what the next few weeks will bring.
I don't know whether everything will become easy.

But I know what you mean to me.

You became someone I cared about deeply.
Someone whose happiness mattered to me.
Someone whose smallest gestures could change my entire day.
Someone I wanted to make memories with.
Someone I wanted to protect, sometimes too much.
Someone I wanted to understand, sometimes too desperately.

And someone I still love.

But now I want to love you differently.

With less fear.
Less control.
More trust.
More patience.
More honesty.
More freedom.

Because if we ever become “us” again, I don't want the same version of us.

I want a healthier one.
A calmer one.
A version where you don't feel trapped and I don't feel terrified.
A version where we both choose each other.
Every day.
Freely.

And if that day comes…

I'll be grateful that after all the confusion,
after all the fear,
after all the distance,

we still found our way back to each other.
— Ayush
